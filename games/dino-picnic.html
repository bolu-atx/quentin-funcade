For grown-ups · our pretend picnic

These are make-believe dinosaurs eating people food. Real dinosaur diets were different; this game is about variety, not a guide to paleontology or exact nutrition. Build a small pretend plate with a fruit or vegetable, a grain, and a protein. Plant eaters have plant-based choices; meat eaters have meat and fish choices. The two menus offer examples of Chinese and American lunches, with many foods enjoyed across cultures. Tofu and pork dumplings count as both grain (wrapper) and protein (filling); add a fruit or vegetable for variety. Plant-eater noodles, wrappers, and treats are made without animal ingredients in this pretend picnic. One optional treat fits too. Two or more treats give our character a pretend tummy wobble, with a kind invitation to change the plate. No food is called “bad,” and there is no score for weight, calories, or eating more. Children’s needs and tolerances vary. There is no need to finish a real plate when you feel full.
